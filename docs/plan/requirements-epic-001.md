## Epic Story

**Epic Goal**: Create an AI-powered, lightweight Linear-style issue tracker for small software teams (3-15 people) who find Jira too heavy.

**Epic Description**: This epic encompasses the core functionality needed to support small development teams in tracking their work without the complexity of enterprise tools. The epic covers authentication and team workspace management, task management on a board, and team collaboration features including comments and activity feeds.

**Key Outcomes**:
- Team members can securely access their workspace
- Teams can track work visually on a Kanban board
- Team members can collaborate through comments and activity feeds

**Business Value**:
- Enables simple, efficient task tracking for small teams
- Reduces tool-switching by providing Linear-style interface
- Improves team visibility and accountability

**Sprint Goal**: Complete MVP with all three core features working in production

**Definition of Done**:
- All three stories implemented and tested
- AI suggestions working for priority and categorization
- 99% uptime for deployment
- Simple onboarding (<5 minutes for team setup)
- All acceptance criteria verified
